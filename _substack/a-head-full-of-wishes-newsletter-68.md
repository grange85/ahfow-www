### Another newly digitised Galaxie 500 show on the Internet Archive

Another Galaxie 500 show is being shared on the Internet Archive. This one is part of [The Jeff Lazar Audio Collection](https://archive.org/details/LazArchives), another taper whose extensive collection is currently being made available.

This is from a show that I didn't have listed in my database, recorded at The Knitting Factory in NYC and apparently the last of four sets the band played over two nights at the venue.

[Galaxie 500 at Knitting Factory on 1990-04-06](https://archive.org/details/galaxie-500-1990-04-06)

    Total running time  [59:49]

    01  Here She Comes Now
    02  Decomposing Trees
    03  Snowstorm
    04  Don't Let Our Youth Go to Waste
    05  Summertime
    06  Melt Away
    07  Temperature's Rising
    08  When Will You Come Home
    09  Victory Garden
    10  Ceremony
    -- encore --
    11  Pictures
    
    
    Band line-up:
    Dean Wareham - guitar, vocals
    Naomi Yang - bass, vocals
    Damon Krukowski - drums
    
    Notes:
    The band played two sets (9PM and midnight) on each of two consecutive nights. This was the midnight set on the second night.

A few weeks back [a couple of previously unavailable recordings](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/2026/06/13/two-newly-digitised-galaxie-500-shows-available-to-download/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260821) were shared as part of the Aadam Jacobs Collection.

### My record collection [379] The Wondrous World of Damon & Naomi (Sub Pop CD)

In late 2006 Damon & Naomi announced a sale in their shop which included 24 copies of the out of print Sub Pop edition of Wondrous World. SHamefully until that point the only copy I had of their second album was the [radio promo picked up in a London record shop](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/2024/06/24/my-record-collection-147-the-wondrous-world-of-damon-naomi-promo-cd/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260821) so I took the opportunity to correct that.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/damon-and-naomi-wondrous-world-sub-pop-cd.jpg" "The Wondrous World of Damon & Naomi (Sub Pop CD)" %}

I also ordered a Galaxie 500 T-shirt, to make the shipping more acceptable. I emailed Naomi and admitted my Wondrous World shame, although since this is now the fifth copy of Wondrous World in this collection I guess I've somewhat redeemed myself?

The booklet is a beautiful piece of Naomi's art and design although that art and design does sometimes make the lyrics hard to read. The insert that came with [the LP](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/2025/12/04/my-record-collection-the-wondrous-world-of-damon-naomi-lp/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260821) was missing most of the booklet but did have readable lyrics.

I rather love the (tiny) pic of Damon & Naomi sitting under a tree and looking so folky on the back of the booklet. I happen to know however that Damon is not so keen on it.

The artwork that was used in the booklet was included in an exhibition of Naomi's art called Victorian Girls in 2016. I didn't make the exhibition (it being so far away) but did get a signed copy of the beautiful booklet.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/naomi-yang-victorian-girls.jpg" "Naomi Yang - Victorian Girls" %}

The CD arrived with a hand-written note from Naomi.

 - *Catalogue Number:* AHFOW 10/115
 - *Artist:* Damon & Naomi
 - *Title:* The Wondrous World of Damon & Naomi
 - *Notes:* Sub Pop edition
 - *Format:* CD
 - Bought from Damon & Naomi in December 2006 for $10
 - **STILL** not available on Bandcamp

### May record collection [380] Britta / Dean - Don''t Think Twice... / Hey Paula

This is my second copy of the split Dean / Britta single released on Great Pop Supplement in 2015. I'm not too sure why I occasionally felt the need to own two copies of certain releases - it seems a bit off having two copies when there were only 400 in total. My 'one for show and one for blow' reasoning doesn't really seem all that sound now. I apologise.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/britta-dont-think-dean-hey-paula.jpg" "Britta / Dean - Don't Think Twice... / Hey Paula" %}

In [the previous post](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/2025/09/11/my-record-collection-dean-britta-don-t-think-twice-hey-paula/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260821) I mostly drifted off on tangents about what constitutes rock and roll and whether a Dean/Britta duet should really be credited as Dean solo (no, btw).

It's a lovely single, with two gorgeous cover versions on it so, not much more to be said. But... [that sleeve](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/database/dean-wareham/releases/singles/#hey-paulagps-131?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260821)... not so grogeous.
