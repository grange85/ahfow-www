### [385] - The Mabuses - self titled LP

The Mabuses were a London band, and this is the first of two albums they released on Rough Trade in the early 90s. Dean guests on backing vocals on tack two, In The Long Run.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/the-mabuses-the-mabuses-lp.jpg" "The Mabuses (LP)" %}

... that track, and most of the album, was recorded by Kramer at Noise New York which I guess explains Dean's appearance. Maybe he just happened to be in the neighbourhood? The album was released in 1991 so dates from around the end of Galaxie 500.

I didn't buy this album at the time, but was aware of The Mabuses (and may well have seen them supporting someone back in the day... although I do think that about a lot of bands, but... I did see a lot of support bands in the early 90s). When I started the website I think someone sent me a copy of the album on cassette but I only got around to buying a copy last year.

### [386] - Dean Wareham - Anesthesia (No. 6 single)

This is a copy of the No. 6 edition of Dean Wareham's first post-Galaxie 500 release Anesthesia. It only arrived in my collection a couple of weeks ago when a Discogs seller in the UK had a copy.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/dean-wareham-anesthesia-no-6.jpg" "Dean Wareham - Anesthesia / I Can't Wait (No.6)" %}

This single actually has a very confused existence as follows:

The sticker on the front of the sleeve declares that the b-side is called "I Don't Care" - it isn't. The label does have the correct title for the b-side although initial pressings didn't actually have the correct b-side. It was released in two vinyl editions, one yellow and one black, and the first pressing had a Dogbowl track called "I Am Drunk Every Night Because of The Blue Fur Bosom Girl". It was then re-pressed in both colours with the correct b-side.

So availabe as:
1. mispressed black vinyl
2. mispressed yellow vinyl
3. correctly pressed black vinyl
4. correctly pressed yellow vinyl

My copy is No.3. I never owned this before because I already had the lovely [Mint Records 12" single](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/2023/09/14/my-record-collection-069-dean-wareham-anesthesia/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260912).

The two songs were the demos Dean recorded at Fun City, produced by Wharton Tiers. Anesthesia with Jimmy Chambers on drums; I Can't Wait with the [late Hamish Kilgour](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/2022/12/05/rip-hamish-kilgour/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=substack&utm_campaign=newsletter+20260912) on drums.

After Lunapark was relased a few months later I settled into the opinion that the demo version of Anesthesia was better than the Luna LP version. I still think that now although I do wonder if this was as much to do with the relief that I mentioned in the earlier Anesthesia posts.

Completist me would of course like copies of 1, 2, and 4.

 - *Catalogue Number:* AHFOW 11/085

 - *Title:* Anesthesia / I Can't Wait
 - *Notes:* black vinyl, correct pressing
 - *Format:* 7"
