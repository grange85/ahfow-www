## The excuse

After a stressful few months, and an even more stressful last week it finally looks like Hazel and I are on the move again. On Monday we'll be heading 60 miles up the coast (not quite reaching Scotland) to our new home in Tweedmouth, the less posh side of the river from Berwick-upon-Tweed.

The week has been chasing solictors and agents trying to get the move confirmed (confirmation only happened on Friday lunchtime - three days before the move). Also... packing, and we have so much stuff! The new house is smaller, I'm not sure how all our junk is going to fit in there.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/substack/substack-trudy-kockenlocker-packed-2026.jpg" "Trudy Kockenlocker - it's not all junk!" %}

We'll have no broadband until Wednesday or Thursday next week and there'll be a lot of unboxing to be done so next week's newsletter, if there is one, will be a similarly rushed affair. Hopefully by the following weekend we'll have started to settle in, and get the hang of it.

Despite all that I did manage to catch a matinee show in Sunderland on Sunday with The Primitives and The Cords. Both were excellent and a perfect temporary distraction.
