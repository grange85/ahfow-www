I've been in London this week visiting family and friends, but that hasn't stopped me keeping an eye on how Luna's US tour is going. One quite exciting development is that the band have added a new cover to their repertoire. "All the People I Like Are Those That Are Dead" was on Felt's 1986 album [Forever Breathes the Lonely Word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forever_Breathes_the_Lonely_Word). Here's a nice clip of Luna playing it in NYC last weekend.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-Io_b2oQ5c

Damon & Naomi play a rare live show as part of [Something in the Hills](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/database/damon-and-naomi/shows/damon-and-naomi-2026-09-26-something-in-the-hills-the-half-moon-hudson-ny-usa/) on Saturday.
