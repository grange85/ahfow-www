# Tugboat / King of Spain

## Aurora Records 7" (AUR 001, 1988)
_1st October 2026_


When I started this series back in January 2023 I never really thought about the end. I just put together a spreadsheet, randomised it, and started picking releases off the top of the list. At some point I started to think that it probably ought to finish with some significance so the last post is about where it all began,

Galaxie 500 recorded Tugboat and King of Spain at Noise New York in February 1988 

<blockquote>
... when we left Noise New York for the first time; we had three songs that we were actually proud of: "Tugboat" and "King of Spain" which became our first single, and "Oblivious", which we gave to that Chemical Imbalance fanzine, which included a 7" single in each issue.
</blockquote>
<p class="caption">Dean Wareham - Temperature's Rising: Galaxie 500 an oral history</p>

It was released on Marc Aghini's _Aurora Records_ which came into existence specifically to release Galaxie 500's first single (and LP). The single was released in May 1988, I'm not sure of the actual date but here are a few dates I am fairly sure of (garnered from the excellent _Temperature's Rising_ book):

 - There's an invoice for production of the single from _Nashville Record Productions_ dated 11th May. Total cost for 1000 copies: $980.22 (that would buy you about eight or nine copies now!)
 - There's a series of pictures of the band, and Marc, numbering the singles dated "May 1988".
 - There's an interview by Francis DiMenno where he mentions being with the band, and the single, on 25th May.

So, given that record release day in the US was on a Tuesday I'd guess that the single was released either on the 24th or 31st May (at a push maybe 17th but that seems unlikely, although it'd be nice if it shared my birthday).

I of course only discovered the band much later so I never owned a copy of the single.

I didn't own a copy of Galaxie 500's first single, I was a year away from being a _fan_ and by the time I was a _collector_ they were priced beyond my reach. At some point in the early 2000s I obviously let slip to Damon and/or Naomi that I didn't have this single, or in fact any Galaxie 500 7" vinyl. Shortly after that a package arrived that fixed that in a spectacular way.
