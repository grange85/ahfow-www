Superchunk have just thrown their hat into the Xmas ring with a five-song EP of mostly covers, one of which is a lively cover of Galaxie 500's Snowstorm.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/misc/superchunk-wintry-mix.jpg" "Superchunk - Wintry Mix EP" %}

The EP has been released as a limited edition double-7" which can be [pre-ordered from their Bandcamp](https://superchunk.bandcamp.com/album/wintry-mix-ep) where you can also grab a digital copy - I'd guess it'll be arriving on the streaming service of your choice soon, if it hasn't already,

As well as the Galaxie 500 cover there's a Superchunk original, and covers of Wham (you know which one) and The Magnetic Fields.
