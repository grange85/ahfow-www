This is my second copy of the split Dean / Britta single released on Great Pop Supplement in 2015. I'm not too sure why I occasionally felt the need to own two copies of certain releases - it seems a bit off having two copies when there were only 400 in total. My 'one for show and one for blow' reasoning doesn't really seem all that sound now. I apologise.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/britta-dont-think-dean-hey-paula.jpg" "Britta / Dean - Don't Think Twice... / Hey Paula" %}

In [the previous post](/2025/09/11/my-record-collection-dean-britta-don-t-think-twice-hey-paula/) I mostly drifted off on tangents about what constitutes rock and roll and whether a Dean/Britta duet should really be credited as Dean solo (no, btw).

It's a lovely single, with two gorgeous cover versions on it so, not much more to be said. But... [that sleeve](/database/dean-wareham/releases/singles/#hey-paulagps-131)... not so grogeous. I'm so prudish that I've manged the two posts without showing the front cover of the sleeve.

 - *Notes:* #336/400
 - *Format:* 7"
