A recording of Magic Hour playing The Knitting Factory in NYC has just been made available to download.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/03-damon_and_naomi/pictures/magic-hour-promo.jpg" "Magic Hour (promo)" %}

Magic Hour was the band that Damon and Naomi formed with Wayne Rogers and Kate Biggar that recorded three albums and played some live shows in the mid/late 90s. Naomi only sang one song for the band, a cover of [Cyril Tawney/Trees Sally Free and Easy](/2014/04/09/originals-sally-free-and-easy-by-trees-covered-by-magic-hour/) and you can hear a live version in this set.

The recording is part of the [collection of taper Jeff Lazar](https://archive.org/details/LazArchives) that is being shared on the Internet Archive:
