Luna head out on a tour of the US that runs from the middle of September into October and in preparation they have made a couple of [new T-shirts available on their website](https://shop.portmerch.com/collections/luna) (and will of course be available at the shows). One is a new design by Rob Carmichael, the second is a new edition of the "Faye Dunaway" shirt that the band first used in 1993.
