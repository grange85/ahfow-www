I must admit that I've felt a little lost since I finished blogging about [my record collection](https://www.fullofwishes.co.uk/category/my-record-collection/), so I've been idling around adding little whistles and bells to AHFoW that only I would probably notice or use.

Over the weekend we had a guest staying. Hannah is a Galaxie 500 fan from London, most of my pals are Galaxie 500 fans (Hannah is the _fan_ who helped make the two AHFoW Galaxie 500 tribute CDs happen). While we were sitting in one evening the conversation turned to what I had been doing on the site recently and I mentioned one of those _whistles_, or _bells_. Since I now have a quite complrehensive database of gigs (partifularly Luna, Dean & Britta, and Damon & Naomi), and a fair few of them have setlists I thought it would be quite interesting to show what songs were being played most frequently.

When I mentioned this Hannah asked the obvious Q... what songs **have** been played most frequently? So...

... at this point in the evening Hazel, whose AHFoW knowledge is all acquired by osmosis, chimed in saying she had no idea what "Knives from Bavaria" was. I played her a version... and then another version, and another. I'm sure she knew it, she had after all seen them play it live in Hebden Bridge a year ago.

So digging through the various versions of Knives from Bavaria reminded me that there was a version of the song that was recorded for the Alison Bagnall film [Piggie](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0368146/fullcredits/?ref_=tt_cst_sm) that Dean acted in back in 2003. I've never seen the film, I keep an eye out for it but it seems very few people have seen it - it has just three reviews on IMDB (all dating from around the time of release), and two on Letterboxd).

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/05-dean_wareham/pictures/dean-wareham-piggie-alison-bagnall-2003-upscale.jpg" "Dean Wareham and Savannah Haske in Piggie (Alison Bagnall, 2003)" %}

The film premiered at the LA Film Festival in June 2003, and played at a few other festivals in the months that followed but has since seemingly disappeared into the ether, if anyone is able to furnish me with a copy of Piggie I'd very much appreciate it!

Since I haven't seen it I scoured the mailing list archives to find out if I knew anyone who did. "Pablo Chang" - a list regular, who suplied lots of pics to the website back then, saw it at the Woodstock Film Festival in September 2003:

<blockquote>
<p>
    I was able to check out PIGGIE at the Bearsville Theater on Fiday night. Good friggin movie! (forgive my eloquence).
</p>

<p>
    It was quite funny, weird, trippy and awkwardly disturbing. Savannah Haske was beautiful, even smeared in pig poo, wearing the oddest mismatch  of country style clothes and the most unsexy pair of pig sloppin rubber boots you can imagine. Even with her unshaven armpits (I thought that was a european thing, must be an upstate NY thing too) she was hot!
</p>

<p>
    Dean was great as an ex-junky, credit card thievin city slicker hiding out up the country. He was perfect, he just seemed so out of place. You had to laugh watching him chain smoke as he was flyfishing and the interactions between him  his  bumpkin landlords and townspeople were very funny.
</p>

<p>
    Theres a scene where Dean almost gets to get it on with a hot waitress in lieu of paying the check (he tried to use a stolen credit card) but is thwarted by the obsessive young Fannie (Savannah' character) banging on the door and carrying on calling him her 'boyfriend' and such.
</p>

<p>
    There was a brief Q&A afterwards with the cast (minus John C Reilly) and it was pretty informative. They talked about how the film was conceived, how the actors were chosen, locations, what formats it was shot in etc.
</p>
 
<p>
    As far as the filming, they certainly captured the green lusciousness  and expansiveness of upstate new york and nailed the sounds of the region (the deafening chatter of the cricket, peepers or whatever the hell makes  that noise all summer?)
</p>

<p>
    I was lucky enough to run into D&B after the show and as always they were so nice to talk with even though they both looked very very tired.
</p>

<p>
    All in all, it was a great movie and a great night.
</p>

<p>
    Thats my 2 cents.
</p>
</blockquote>
<p class="caption">PabloTChang (Galaxie 500 Mailing List - 22nd September 2003)</p>

Someone on the list also mentioned that the lead actress/co-writer Savannah Haske, had been handing out Piggie promo postcards at a Dean & Britta show in LA before the film's showing at the LA Film Festival.

Anyway... where was I? 

Savannah Haske, as well as co-writing the film also wrote some new lyrics for _Knives from Bavaria_. The song was renamed _Uterus_ and Dean & Britta shared it on their website back then.
