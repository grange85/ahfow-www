I've probably said pretty much all there is to be said about how the single of Luna's "Dear Paulina" came about, so I'm not too sure what this post is going to be. I suspect It'll cover a lot of ground that's been in the three earlier posts (see below), the long [lost tracks post](/2009/07/11/mp3-lost-tracks-6-luna-dear-paulina/) I made a few years ago, and the [Substack rehash from last year](https://aheadfullofwishes.substack.com/p/the-making-of-lunas-dear-paulina).

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/luna-dear-paulina-framed-museum.jpg" "Dear Paulina in 'the museum'" %}

Having completed the single, and sold as many as I could I decided that I really ought to have a signed copy of it on my wall so I asked Dean if he could sort that out for me. I had _paid_ the band in copies of the single, so Dean took one around the band, got them to sign it, and sent it back to me. I bought a couple of cheap frames and it has lived on the wall of the three homes we've had since the single was released... and will be on the wall of the fourth soon (I hope).

In May 2017 Luna launched a Pledgemusic campaign to fund the covers album, _A Sentimental Education_ and for one of the _extras_ you could buy, Dean would write out by hand the lyrics of a song of your choice. I initially resisted, it was tricky to justify the cost, but by July the idea of having Dear Paulina written out by Dean hanging next to the signed single seemed perfect, so I just went for it.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/00-misc/my-record-collection/luna-dear-paulina-and-lyric-sheet-signed.jpg" "Signed sleeve and lyric sheet" %}

Now, I know people put out singles all the time, I know it must be a thrill, but also it's what they do. I ran a mailing list and a web page, that's what I did. Putting out a single was a significant event. It wasn't a record label putting out another single, it was a _fan_ putting out a single by a band he loves. Of course labels love their bands... but it's different. When there's another single by another band coming you just get on with it. For me this was a one-off. It was important for me to do it right, and do it as well as I could. And I wanted it to be something that I could look back on and say "wow, I made that happen".

Thousands of records have been cut at Abbey Road, so that "Dear Paulina" was cut at Abbey Road doesn't really mean anything. Except, it was cut at Abbey Road with Hazel and myself sitting on a sofa watching. It's the only record that that happened to&dagger;.

You can probably tell that still, 27 years on, I still look back on "Dear Paulina" with a ridiculous amount of pride.
