The fancy Wax Mage re-issues of Luna's last two albums went on sale yesterday. The bundles sold out pretty quickly but there are still copies of the various Wax Mage variants available from their online shop.

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/02-luna/sleeves/luna-romantica-wax-mage-2026.jpg" "Luna - Romantica (Wax Mage InkSplosion)" %}
{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/02-luna/sleeves/luna-rendezvous-wax-mage-2026.jpg" "Luna - Rendezvous (Wax Mage InkSplosion)" %}

- [Luna - Romantica (Wax Mage exclusive variants)](https://waxmage.com/products/luna-romantica)
- [Luna - Rendezvous (Wax Mage)](https://waxmage.com/products/luna-rendezvous)

Wax Mage do ship internationally although £65 shipping to the UK for a £46 LP is a little much!

A sixth (or seventh?) variant called "InkSplosion" is available from Bandcamp

 - [Luna - Romantica (Physical LP Only - No Download) (Bandcamp)](https://lunabewitched.bandcamp.com/album/romantica-physical-lp-only-no-download)
 - [Luna - Rendezvous (Physical LP Only - No Download) (Bandcamp)](https://lunabewitched.bandcamp.com/album/rendezvous-physical-lp-only-no-download)

_(although at $30 a pop would a download be so hard to include?)_
