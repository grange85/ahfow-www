Dean & Britta will be playing a show in Olympia WA on the 16th August as part of the "Scherler Sundays" free outdoor concert series behind the "historic Carnegie Library" - the show starts at 3pm (local time) and it looks like it'll be [streamed live on their YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tjw5WCamiUU).

{% ahfowimage "https://media.fullofwishes.co.uk/07-dean_and_britta/show_assets/2026/2026-08-16-dean-britta-olympia-wa.jpg" "Scherler Sundays - Olympia WA" %}

<blockquote>
Britta and I going to be playing a free afternoon show in Olympia, WA – Sunday August 16 at 5pm, outdoors at the Historic Carnegie Library. It's my first time ever in Olympia and we'll be playing a set heavy on Galaxie 500 songs, in trio format (with Roger on drums).
</blockquote>
